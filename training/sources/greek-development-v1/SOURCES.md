# Source survey

Rechecked 21 November 2026 for occurrence-level Greek lemma, morphology and dependency annotation. A scholarly text, a list of possible dictionary parses, an automatic tag, and an editor’s choice for a particular token provide different kinds of evidence. The distributed files, eligibility decisions, source revisions and conversion policy are described in the [collection guide](README.md).

| Source investigated | Evidence and acquisition outcome |
|---|---|
| University of Helsinki, PapyGreek, Zenodo, Papyri.info and Sematia | Helsinki publishes original and regularized annotations with named, layer-specific review metadata. Archived all 558 documentary XML files and exported the eligible native subset. Papyri.info supplies the editions. Older Sematia material was incorporated into PapyGreek. Treat duplicate catalogue entries as the same work. [Treebank archive](https://zenodo.org/records/8428823) · [Project publication](https://doi.org/10.5334/johd.55) · [Underlying editions](https://github.com/papyri/idp.data) |
| Digital Hill and Perseids publication collections | Digital Hill publishes one annotated Euripides XML in its repository. Retrieved it and checked all three verse lines. The UNESP *Bacchae* passage belongs to the existing locked test. Pedalion’s *Medea* and *Exagoge* are represented in v3 training. [Digital Hill](https://github.com/DigitalHill/treebank-data) · [UNESP](https://github.com/perseids-publications/unesp-trees) · [Pedalion](https://github.com/perseids-publications/pedalion-trees) |
| Daphne, both the maintained collection and its older mirror | The maintained catalogue supplies two previously overlooked complete work files. Source review status is per work. Whole-work Greek alignment resolves the *Ion* title discrepancy. The older mirror alone misses these records. [Maintained catalogue](https://github.com/francescomambrini/Daphne/tree/06aa1f27a34af98bba1407dc95fe2085a72f8b7f/data) · [Earlier mirror](https://github.com/perseids-publications/daphne-trees) |
| Perseus AGDT, Gorman and Pedalion | Retrieved 12 scholarly AGDT tragedy files and the two relevant Pedalion tragedy files. Matched their work filenames to 8,928 shipped supervision rows. They could support a separately trained, play-excluded model. The training-source inventory also guards documentary overlap. [AGDT](https://github.com/PerseusDL/treebank_data) · [Gorman](https://github.com/vgorman1/Greek-Dependency-Trees) · [Pedalion](https://github.com/perseids-publications/pedalion-trees) |
| LASLA, University of Liège and Hyperbase | Liège’s Greek catalogue lists all seven surviving plays of Sophocles, seven of Aeschylus and the Euripidean corpus, including fragments in separate entries. The Hyperbase Euripides catalogue reports 161,455 occurrences and 19 works, including the satyr play *Cyclops*. Georges Rigo’s lemmatized Euripides index substantiates the lemma lead. The verified public result was catalogue metadata. Hyperbase’s consultation/export route returned an authentication redirect during this audit. The advertised open LASLA corpus download covers Latin. A Greek occurrence-by-occurrence export and its annotation columns remain to be obtained. No dependency-tree availability was established. [Liège catalogue](https://www.lasla.uliege.be/cms/c_8570498/fr/lasla-textes-grecs) · [Liège project history](https://www.lasla.uliege.be/cms/c_8570944/fr/lasla-textes-grecs) · [Hyperbase corpus](http://hyperbase.unice.fr/euripide) · [LASLA corpora study](https://doi.org/10.1484/J.EUPHR.5.125282) |
| GLAUx, OGA and derived Greek XML collections | Inspected sentence-level analysis provenance in the available GLAUx tragedy files. Complete candidate works including *Philoctetes*, *Oedipus at Colonus* and *Iphigenia in Tauris* carry automatic analyses. Reused manual portions trace to existing treebanks. OGA-derived exports and the automated Lemmatized Ancient Greek XML corpus also require source-specific provenance before editorial use. [GLAUx and its listed manual sources](https://github.com/alekkeersmaekers/glaux) · [Lemmatized Ancient Greek XML](https://github.com/gcelano/LemmatizedAncientGreekXML) |
| Diorisis, canonical Greek editions, First1KGreek and GreekDraCor | Diorisis supplies broad lemma coverage, with documented manually checked samples in Homer and Lysias. Canonical Greek editions and drama corpora provide work identity, reference text and structure. These were useful for alignment and are potential sources of unlabeled text. [Diorisis](https://figshare.com/articles/The_Diorisis_Ancient_Greek_Corpus/6187256) · [Canonical editions](https://github.com/PerseusDL/canonical-greekLit) · [First1KGreek](https://github.com/OpenGreekAndLatin/First1KGreek) · [GreekDraCor](https://github.com/dracor-org/greekdracor) |
| Teaching editions, metric corpora and discovery services | Checked classroom treebank repositories, the Furman *Oedipus* edition, Perseus under PhiloLogic, Hypotactic, and CLARIN’s drama records, alongside GitHub and Zenodo catalogues. The relevant alternatives encountered offered possible morphological analyses, mixed provenance, meter, or drama alignment. No additional export with demonstrably human-selected per-occurrence lemma or dependency labels on an eligible play was verified through these routes. [Harrington treebanks](https://github.com/perseids-publications/harrington-trees) · [Hypotactic](https://hypotactic.com/uncategorized/treebanks-tragedy-and-standoff-xml/) · [CLARIN:EL](https://inventory.clarin.gr/) |

Ellendt’s digitized *Lexicon Sophocleum* is a further source for editorial cross-checks of particular Greek lemmas. Its print citations and OCR require edition alignment and manual word identification. [Digitized lexicon](https://archive.org/details/lexiconsophocleu00elleuoft)

The highest-yield remaining acquisition is an occurrence-level LASLA export for a work outside v3 training. The shortest route to additional syntax evidence is a documented independent review of eligible passages already present in the two Daphne files. Record reviewer names, reviewed scope, source and target schemes, disagreements, adjudications and work identity before promoting any passage.
